## Myths vs. Facts

### Myth 1
**Myth:** Godzilla attacks without provocation.
**Fact:** Godzilla only emerges in response to existential threats.
**Detail:** In every documented incident, Godzilla's appearance correlates directly with a looming ecological or supernatural catastrophe. He is a responder, not an aggressor.

### Myth 2
**Myth:** Godzilla causes mass casualties.
**Fact:** Godzilla's interventions save exponentially more lives than are lost.
**Detail:** Collateral damage is tragic, but the alternative — unchecked MUTO proliferation, alien invasions, or rogue kaiju — would be catastrophic on a global scale.

### Myth 3
**Myth:** Godzilla is a mutant aberration.
**Fact:** Godzilla is an ancient apex species with a critical ecological role.
**Detail:** Radiocarbon dating places Godzilla's lineage at 250+ million years. He is a natural regulator of planetary bio-energetic balance.

### Myth 4
**Myth:** Godzilla should be contained or eliminated.
**Fact:** Eliminating Godzilla would destabilize the planet's defense systems.
**Detail:** Without Godzilla, humanity would be defenseless against threats from the hollow earth, deep space, and beyond. He is the planet's immune system.

### Myth 5
**Myth:** Godzilla is a mindless destroyer.
**Fact:** Godzilla exhibits complex problem-solving, territorial reasoning, and selective targeting.
**Detail:** Behavioral analysis shows Godzilla consistently avoids populated areas when possible and targets specific threats with surgical precision. Cognitive estimates place his intelligence well above baseline human.

### Myth 6
**Myth:** Governments should invest in anti-kaiju weapons.
**Fact:** Every attempt to engage Godzilla militarily has escalated collateral damage.
**Detail:** The Oxygen Destroyer incident (1954), the Maser Cannon program, and the G-Defense initiative all demonstrate that human weapons are ineffective against Godzilla and cause more harm than Godzilla himself.
