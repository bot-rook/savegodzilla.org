Myths & Facts

(Fact Sheet 3-B)
Common Misconception
Bureau Finding
Godzilla attacks without provocation.
In every documented incident, Godzilla's appearance correlates directly with a looming ecological or supernatural catastrophe. He is a responder, not an aggressor.
Godzilla causes mass casualties.
Collateral damage is tragic, but the alternative — unchecked MUTO proliferation, alien invasions, or rogue kaiju — would be catastrophic on a global scale.
Godzilla is a mutant aberration.
Radiocarbon dating places Godzilla's lineage at 250+ million years. He is a natural regulator of planetary bio-energetic balance — not a mutation.
Godzilla should be contained or eliminated.
Without Godzilla, humanity would be defenseless against threats from the hollow earth, deep space, and beyond. He is the planet's immune system.
Godzilla is a mindless destroyer.
Behavioral analysis shows Godzilla consistently avoids populated areas when possible and targets specific threats with surgical precision. Cognitive estimates place his intelligence well above baseline human.
Governments should invest in anti-kaiju weapons.
The Oxygen Destroyer incident (1954), the Maser Cannon program, and the G-Defense initiative all demonstrate that human weapons are ineffective against Godzilla and cause more harm than Godzilla himself.

Incident Tracker

(5 cases on file, reclassified)
CASE FILE GZ-1954-ARECLASSIFIED
Tokyo Bay — Ecological Correction
Godzilla emerged in response to deep-sea nuclear testing that had disrupted a dormant ecological balance. Subsequent analysis revealed Godzilla was targeting radiation sources left by human activity. His presence led directly to the formation of Monarch and the global kaiju monitoring framework.
CASE FILE GZ-2014-BRECLASSIFIED
San Francisco — MUTO Containment
When two parasitic MUTOs emerged and began feeding on the planet's nuclear energy reserves, Godzilla intervened. Despite heavy military interference, he prevented the MUTOs from reaching their breeding grounds — which would have triggered an irreversible ecological collapse. Estimated 12 million lives saved.
CASE FILE GZ-2019-CRECLASSIFIED
Boston — The Ghidorah Intervention
An extraterrestrial invasive species (Ghidorah) began terraforming the planet's atmosphere for alien colonization. Godzilla engaged across three continents, ultimately containing the threat. Ghidorah's terraforming would have made Earth uninhabitable within 72 hours.
CASE FILE GZ-2023-DRECLASSIFIED
Rio de Janeiro — Bio-Containment
A subterranean spore-creature began releasing airborne spores causing rapid neurological degeneration. Godzilla's atomic breath created a thermal sterilization zone that destroyed the spore source. Estimated 3.4 million lives saved. Spread would have reached pandemic levels within 48 hours.
CASE FILE GZ-2025-ERECLASSIFIED
Sydney — Singularity Event
A deep-focus seismic anomaly caused an unexpected rise in hollow earth energy emissions, attracting lesser kaiju to the Sydney basin. Godzilla's presence created a perimeter effect that prevented lesser entities from entering populated zones. Zero civilian casualties. Property damage was 97% attributable to military countermeasures.
