Myths vs. Facts

The media has spent decades weaponizing misinformation. Here's the reality.

Godzilla attacks without provocation.
Godzilla only emerges in response to existential threats.

In every documented incident, Godzilla's appearance correlates directly with a looming ecological or supernatural catastrophe. He is a responder, not an aggressor.

Godzilla causes mass casualties.
Godzilla's interventions save exponentially more lives than are lost.

Collateral damage is tragic, but the alternative — unchecked MUTO proliferation, alien invasions, or rogue kaiju — would be catastrophic on a global scale.

Godzilla is a mutant aberration.
Godzilla is an ancient apex species with a critical ecological role.

Radiocarbon dating places Godzilla's lineage at 250+ million years. He is a natural regulator of planetary bio-energetic balance — not a mutation.

Godzilla should be contained or eliminated.
Eliminating Godzilla would destabilize the planet's defense systems.

Without Godzilla, humanity would be defenseless against threats from the hollow earth, deep space, and beyond. He is the planet's immune system.
